# SC-100 (Plaintiff's Claim and ORDER to Go to Small Claims Court) — item guide

Form revision: Rev. January 1, 2026 (6 pages; the plaintiff fills pages 2–4). Field ids
below are suffixes of the AcroForm ids (full ids start `SC-100[0].PageN[0]...`); the
deterministic map is `assets/sc100_field_map.json`.

## Who fills what

| Area | Filled by | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Page 1 "Clerk stamps date here", Case Number, Case Name | Court | Leave empty. |
| Page 1 "Order to Go to Court" (trial date/time/department/court address 1–3, clerk date and signature) | Clerk | Leave empty. |
| Page 1 "Fill in court name and street address" (`CourtInfo`) | Plaintiff | Only if the case names the court and its address; otherwise empty. |
| Pages 2–4 caption "Plaintiff (list names)" | Plaintiff | Plaintiff name(s). Caption "Case Number" stays empty (court assigns it). |
| Pages 5–6 "Need help?" small-claims-advisor boxes | Court/county | Leave empty. |
| Print / Save / Clear buttons | — | Never touch. |

## Items

1. **Plaintiff** — name, phone, street, city, state, zip; mailing address only "if different"; email "if available". A second plaintiff goes in the second block. More than two plaintiffs: check the box and attach **SC-100A**. Doing business under a fictitious name: check and attach **SC-103**. Payday lender / "licensee" or "deferred deposit originator" under Financial Code §§ 23000 et seq.: check that box.
2. **Defendant** — name, phone, street, city, state, zip; mailing only if different. If the defendant is a corporation, LLC, or public entity, list the person/agent authorized for service of process (name, job title if known, address); leave that block empty for an individual defendant. More than one defendant: check and attach **SC-100A**. Defendant on active military duty: check the box (and write the name by hand; there is no text field).
3. **Claim** — amount the defendant owes (the "$" is printed; write the number). 3a why the defendant owes it. 3b when it happened: a specific date, or if none, "Date started" and "Through". 3c how the amount was calculated (do not include court costs or fees for service). If more space is needed, check the box and attach one sheet of paper or form **MC-031** with "SC-100, Item 3" at the top.
4. **Asked to pay first** — the plaintiff must ask the defendant (in person, in writing, or by phone) to pay — or, for a claim for possession of property, to give back the property — before suing. Yes / No; if No, explain why not. Texts, emails, letters, and calls count as asking.
5. **Why this courthouse** — check exactly one:
   - a. (1) where the defendant lives or does business; (2) where the plaintiff's property was damaged; (3) where the plaintiff was injured; (4) where a contract (written or spoken) was made, signed, performed, or broken by the defendant, or where the defendant lived or did business when the defendant made the contract.
   - b. where the buyer or lessee signed the contract, lives now, or lived when the contract was made — claim about an offer or contract for personal, family, or household goods, services, or loans (Code Civ. Proc. § 395(b)).
   - c. where the buyer signed the contract, lives now, or lived when the contract was made — retail installment contract such as a credit card (Civ. Code § 1812.10).
   - d. same as c, or where the vehicle is permanently garaged — vehicle finance sale (Civ. Code § 2984.4).
   - e. other (specify).
6. **ZIP of the place checked in 5** — "if you know" (filing where the defendant lives → defendant's ZIP).
7. **Attorney-client fee dispute?** Yes / No. If yes and there has been arbitration, fill out **SC-101**, attach it, and check the box.
8. **Suing a public entity?** Yes / No. If yes, a written claim must be filed with the entity first; check the box and give the date it was filed. The SC-100 can be filed once the entity denies the claim or does not answer within the time allowed by law.
9. **More than 12 other small claims in California in the last 12 months?** Yes / No (yes means a higher filing fee). "First time suing" → No.
10. **Claim over $2,500?** Yes / No — strictly greater than $2,500. Answering yes also confirms the plaintiff has not filed, and understands they may not file, more than two small claims over $2,500 in California during the calendar year.
11. **No right to appeal** — acknowledgment that filing in small claims gives the plaintiff no right to appeal the claim; no field.

**Declaration** (under penalty of perjury that the information is true and correct) — "Date" and "Plaintiff types or prints name here" for plaintiff 1; the second date/name line only for a second plaintiff. Signatures are by hand (no field).

**Requests for accommodations** (page 4 notice): assistive listening, real-time captioning, or sign-language interpreters are available if asked at least five days before trial; contact the clerk for form MC-410 (Civ. Code § 54.8). Informational only; no field.

## Filing context (from the page 1 instructions; not form fields)

- Read form SC-100-INFO first (courthouse, county law library, or courts.ca.gov/rules-forms/find-your-court-forms). Fill out pages 2, 3, and 4. Copy all pages and attachments — one for each party plus one for the plaintiff — then take or mail the original and copies to the clerk and pay the filing fee; the clerk writes the trial date. Some courts allow electronic filing (courts.ca.gov/find-my-court.htm).
- Someone at least 18 who is not listed in the case must give each defendant a court-stamped copy of all pages and attachments ("serving"); special rules apply to public entities, associations, and some businesses (SC-104, SC-104B, SC-104C).
- Notice to the defendant (page 1): both parties must go to court on the trial date; a defendant who does not appear may lose, and the court can order wages, money, or property taken to pay the claim. Bring witnesses, receipts, and any evidence.
